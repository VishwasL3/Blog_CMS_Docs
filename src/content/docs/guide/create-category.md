title: 7. Create Category
description: Go to the Categories Page, there you can see all categories.
---

- Go to the Categories Page, there you can see all categories.
- Click the "Add Category" button to access the category creation form. Fill in the form, and your category will be created. 
- You can Update and Delete your Custom Category.
