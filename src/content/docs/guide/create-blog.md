title: 3. Creating a New Blog Post
description: Learn how to create, preview, save drafts, and publish new blog posts from the CMS dashboard.
---

To create a new blog post, go to the **Blogs** page and click the **New Blog** button.

## Steps to Create

1. **Fill in required details** (title, category, tags, etc.).
2. Add your blog content in the built-in editor.
3. Choose from three options:
   - **Preview**: See how your blog will appear before publishing.
   - **Save as Draft**: Store your blog for future editing (not visible to the public).
   - **Publish**: Make the blog live for everyone to read.
